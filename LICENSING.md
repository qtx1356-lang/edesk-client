# Licensing checklist

The upstream RustDesk repositories use AGPL-3.0. Before distributing a modified eDesk build:
- Read the exact LICENSE at the pinned commit.
- Preserve required copyright and license notices.
- Meet applicable source-code and build-instruction obligations.
- Audit bundled dependencies and their licenses.
- Review RustDesk trademark and logo use separately.
- Do not imply eDesk is an official RustDesk product.

This is project guidance, not legal advice.
